Privacy policy

Your journal
stays with you.

The app stores your workouts locally. This page also explains what happens when you export records, contact support, visit this website or use TestFlight.

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Current app: version 1.0.0

Lift & Hop — Gym Journal is published by Akitso-I. In this policy, “we” means the publisher. Contact lift-hop@outlook.com with privacy questions or requests.

1. Your journal on your iPhone

Folders, exercises, workout dates, sets, weights, repetitions and cardio durations are stored locally on your device. Theme and history-layout preferences are also stored on the device.

The app works offline, requires no app account and does not provide live journal synchronization. The current app has no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not automatically send your journal or preferences to us.

2. Exports, recovery files and backups

You choose when to export a CSV and where to save or share it. Exports contain saved journal records, including folder and exercise names. They do not contain unsaved drafts or display preferences.

Before an Add or Replace import, the app saves a recovery CSV of the existing journal on the same device. Recovery files remain after relaunch and are not automatically deleted. Editing or deleting a record in the current journal does not remove copies already present in recovery files or exports.

Local recovery files are not an off-device backup. You control exported copies and the locations or services you choose to store them in. Device backups may also contain app data, depending on your Apple settings.

3. Sharing with other services

The app provides optional text prompts for use with external AI tools, but does not automatically send your journal to an AI service. A prompt file alone contains no personal journal data.

If you share a CSV, screenshot or other records with an AI tool, cloud-storage provider, email recipient or another service, that recipient handles the information under its own privacy practices. Share only information you intend the recipient to receive.

4. Support email

If you contact lift-hop@outlook.com, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you provide. We use these only to answer your request and investigate app issues.

We delete unnecessary attachments when the issue is resolved and retain other correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you have sent. Please avoid sending personal workout records unless they are needed to explain the issue.

The support mailbox uses Microsoft Outlook.com. Microsoft also processes email under its Privacy Statement.

5. Visits to this website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or contact forms to this site. Images and styles are served with the website; no third-party font or analytics service is loaded.

6. TestFlight beta testing

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash reports and usage information and shares them with the developer. This can include app version, device and operating-system details, sessions and crashes. Your invitation method can also determine which identifying details the developer receives.

Feedback you submit can include comments and screenshots. Avoid including personal workout details you do not want to share. This TestFlight handling is separate from the app’s local journal storage. For details and Apple’s controls, see TestFlight & Privacy.

7. Your choices

You control the journal on your device and the exports you create. We cannot access or remotely delete your local journal. To ask about information you have sent to support or through TestFlight, email lift-hop@outlook.com.

Information held by Apple, Microsoft and GitHub is also subject to their policies and privacy controls. Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information. In Finland, information about these rights and complaints is available from the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app or its supporting services change how they handle information. The date above identifies the latest update.

For help using the app, visit Support or the user guide.
